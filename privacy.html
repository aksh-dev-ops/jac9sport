This Privacy Policy explains what information SportZone collects when you visit our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Introduction

SportZone ("we", "us", "our") provides live scores, fixtures, sports news and related content. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

This policy applies to all pages of our website and any related services that link to it.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details, such as your email address when you subscribe to our newsletter.
  • Messages you send us through contact forms or email.
  • Preferences, such as your favourite sports or teams, if you choose to set them.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, operating system and screen size.
  • IP address and approximate location (country or city).
  • Pages you visit, time spent on the site and links you click.
  • Referring website or advertisement that brought you to us.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide and improve the website and its content.
  • To send newsletters and match alerts you have signed up for.
  • To respond to your questions and requests.
  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can make it better.
  • To show relevant content and advertising.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse and security problems.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link at the bottom of every message.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and browser storage to make the site work and to understand how it is used. For example, we may remember that you have confirmed your age so we don't ask again.

  • Essential cookies keep the site running and secure.
  • Analytics cookies help us count visits and see which pages are popular.
  • Advertising cookies may be set by our partners to show relevant ads and measure their performance.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work properly without them.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it only with:

  • Service providers who help us run the site, such as hosting, email and analytics providers, under contracts that protect your data.
  • Advertising partners, limited to non-identifying or pseudonymous data.
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.
  • A new owner, if SportZone is merged with or sold to another company.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, or as required by law. Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is kept in aggregated or anonymised form.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and restricted access, to protect your information. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
  • Object to or restrict how we use it.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent.
  • Receive a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Age restriction

This website is intended only for people aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us their information, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Third-party links

Our site may contain links to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites. Please read their privacy policies before sharing any information with them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes may also be announced on the site or by email.
